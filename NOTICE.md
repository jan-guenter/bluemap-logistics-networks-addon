# Notices

This project is an independently authored MIT BlueMap add-on.

Its entrypoint, exact-artifact detector, registration boundary, archive gate,
and low-level cuboid emission structure adapt MIT patterns from the owner's
released BlueMap Pipez and Integrated Dynamics add-ons. The LogisticsNetworks
frame topology and resource-model admission policy are new to this project.

It interoperates with BlueMap 5.22 (MIT) through a compile-only internal API.
LogisticsNetworks remains operator-installed and is not bundled. Its exact
1.10.1 runtime artifact declares `All Rights Reserved`; no LogisticsNetworks
source, classes, models, textures, meshes, or algorithms are distributed by
this project.
