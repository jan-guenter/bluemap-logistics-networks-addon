# Changelog

## 0.1.0-SNAPSHOT

- Add the bounded exact-profile LogisticsNetworks node-rendering prototype.
- Admit bounded weighted blockstate choices only when every outcome proves the
  same ordinary full-cube occupied geometry.
- Supersede the earlier half-height clean-room hypothesis from new owner
  screenshots: use a one-block-tall cage over local `y=1/2..3/2`, while
  retaining provisional `1/8` rails, `1/256` outset, and planar frame UVs.
- Generate a pale-neutral semi-transparent 1-by-1 top-sheet texture in memory;
  reserve and verify its synthetic key and fail closed on collision or error.
- Emit one upward aperture sheet per node and merge only evidenced cardinal
  surfaces by removing shared upper edges plus doubly connected upper corners
  and their vertical posts.
- Recess the sheet by the authored `1/256` outset below the top-rail underside
  so retained frames and sheets have no positive-area coplanar faces.
- Add one outward-wound translucent pane to each exposed cardinal aperture,
  omit it at connected interfaces, and reuse the exact generated sheet
  material. Perpendicular neighbors extend exterior panes to their exact cell
  boundary; an authored `1/256` safety inset avoids positive-area pane/frame
  and pane/pane coplanarity without changing frame/post topology.
- Remove the four internal raised corner posts exposed by a project-owned
  2-by-2 staging render while retaining straight and L perimeter posts.
- Require exact air above every admitted host; keep vertical masks capped and
  closed without invented bridges.
- Exhaustively test all 16 horizontal masks, rotation, exact geometry counts,
  exposed side count and winding, coverage, coplanarity,
  isolated/straight/L/2-by-2 topology, texture
  lifecycle, headroom, capacity, and pass-local atomic rollback.
- Resolve frame and sheet material IDs from their stable keys at every emit so
  BlueMap texture-gallery purge/repopulation cannot leave cached stale IDs;
  missing or aliased post-reset mappings fail before model mutation.
